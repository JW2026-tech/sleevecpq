Seller's obligation under the Warranty is to supply, free of charge, a replacement part for any part of the Equipment that fails due to a covered defect during the 60-month Warranty Period. Most parts are straightforward to replace and are installed by Buyer's own personnel; in these cases, Seller's obligation is limited to shipping the replacement part.

Where a repair or replacement genuinely requires Seller's service engineers to travel to site, Seller bears the associated labor and travel costs, provided the failure is confirmed to be a covered defect under the Warranty. If it is later determined that the failure was caused by Buyer, including but not limited to any of the circumstances described in Article 7.4.2, Buyer shall bear the full cost of such labor and travel, regardless of who requested or arranged the visit.

Parts supplied by third parties are warranted only for the period granted by the relevant third-party manufacturer to Seller, passed through to Buyer on a back-to-back basis, regardless of the Warranty period.

Seller's liability under the Warranty does not apply to:

  • Modifications to the Works by Buyer or third party without prior written approval by Seller.
  • Normal wear and tear of parts.
